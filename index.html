With no wind, every beach is good today. Our pick is Praia Grande, 200 m from here: stay until late and watch the sunset right there at 18:14. The water is warm (27°C), no cold upwelling today. Heads up though: 63% chance of rain. Bring a raincoat and keep an eye on the sky.

Sin viento, todas las playas están buenas hoy. Nuestra recomendación es Praia Grande, a 200 m de aquí: quédate hasta tarde y mira el atardecer ahí mismo, a las 18:14.
